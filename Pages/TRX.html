TRX

TRX is an open source engine for Tomb Raider I-II-II.

It works on also on macOS, on both Intel and Apple Silicon.

Check out the repo

Installing

Download from the releases of the repo, the DMG of latest TRX

Mount the DMG and drag TRX app in Applications

Once installed, you'll need original Tomb Raider game files, you must provide them from an instance or a game disc.
